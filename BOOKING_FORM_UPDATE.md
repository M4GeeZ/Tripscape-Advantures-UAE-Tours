# Booking form update

- Replaced the non-bookable TourDetail **Contact for price / Ask on WhatsApp** sidebar with a booking enquiry form.
- Added name, age, travel date, adults, children, email, phone, and WhatsApp availability fields.
- Added dynamic **WhatsApp + Email / Send by Email** primary action and **Call Now** secondary action.
- Added `/api/bookings` email endpoint that sends both the company booking notification and customer confirmation.
- Existing fixed-price tour price calculator is unchanged.
