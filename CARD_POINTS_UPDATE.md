# Card Points Update

- Tour/service card descriptions are displayed as feature points only.
- Compact homepage and image-heavy service cards show 5 points.
- Larger tour/category listing cards show up to 7 points.
- Existing tour detail pages and their Description / Included / Excluded / FAQ content were not changed.
- The `Guided tour` label on City Tour image cards is now white for better visibility; this color change is scoped only to City Tour cards.
