Birthday Party Desert Setup

Description

Turn a birthday into a private desert celebration with reserved seating, dinner and a comfortable setup for family or friends. The package can be shaped around your guest count, preferred timing and any optional birthday extras you want to add.

What's included

  • Private birthday seating setup
  • Reserved area for your group
  • Dinner arrangement as confirmed
  • Welcome refreshments
  • Desert photo stop
  • Dedicated guest support
  • WhatsApp event coordination

What's excluded

  • Birthday cake unless confirmed
  • Custom balloons, flowers or themed decoration unless selected
  • Professional photography unless selected
  • Premium entertainment upgrades
  • Anything not listed in the final booking confirmation

Frequently asked questions

Can you arrange a birthday cake?

A cake can be requested as an optional extra. Share the size, flavour and message required before booking.

Can we add decorations?

Yes. Decoration requests are subject to availability and are quoted separately.

Is this suitable for families?

Yes. The setup can be planned for families and small private groups, subject to the final guest count.

How do I receive the final price?

Send your date, guest count and requested extras through WhatsApp for a tailored quote.
