Common questions

Which desert safari is best for first-time visitors?

An evening safari is popular because it usually combines dune driving, sunset views, camp activities and dinner in one experience.

How far in advance should I book?

Booking ahead is recommended for weekends, holidays and peak travel months.

Are all desert safari activities included?

No. Inclusions vary by package, so always check the exact activity list before booking.
